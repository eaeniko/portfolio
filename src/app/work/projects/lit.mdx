## My life project. A productivity gamefied superapp.


### **Hi! I'm Niko, and I'm building the app of our dreams.**

Ever feel... overwhelmed?

You know that feeling of having a thousand things you *want* to do—learn to code, hit the gym, read that book, clean your room—but the motivation just isn't there?

For many of us, especially those dealing with anxiety, depression, ADHD, or just feeling alone on the journey, standard to-do lists aren't a solution. They're just **one more problem.**

They're boring. They're tedious. And after a few days, they feel more like a boss yelling at you than a tool to help you. So, we quit.

I know this because I live this. And that's why I decided to build **LIT**.


### **The Mission: Life. Improved. Together.**

I searched for an app that did what I dreamed of:

* A task app that was **clean, minimalist, and ADHD-friendly**.
* With the hardcore, meaningful progression of a **`Solo Leveling`** RPG (where going to the gym *actually* raises your `Strength` stat).
* With the deep, "set-it-and-forget-it" idle skilling of **`Melvor Idle`** or **`Gladiatus`**.
* With the cozy, social, and decorative side of **`Animal Crossing`**.
* With a fair, cosmetic-only economy like **`Path of Exile`**.
* And, in the future, could help me learn like **`Duolingo`** and explore the real world like **`Walkscape`**.

I couldn't find it. So, on **October 18th, 2025** (the day of our first concept discussion!), I decided to build it.

**LIT** isn't just another task app. It's a **Gamified Productivity Superapp** designed to be the "operating system" for your life.


### **What is LIT? (The Grand Vision)**

LIT is a hybrid project: a full-featured productivity app and a deep 2D game, all in one. It's built on two layers:

#### **Plan 1: The Free Plan (A 100% OFFLIaE Single-Player Game)**

This is not a "demo." It's a complete, fast, and private game that is 100% free, forever.

* **Full PIM:** A robust task app with sub-tasks, notes, native calendar sync, a flexible Pomodoro timer, and reminders.
* **Stats (`Solo Leveling`):** Track your real-life activities (exercise, study, sleep) to level up your Avatar's core stats (`STR`, `INT`, `AGI`, `VIT`).
* **Idle Hub (`Melvor` + `Gladiatus`):** Use "Idle Fuel" (from tasks) for passive skilling (Mining, Crafting) and "Focus Tickets" (from Pomodoro) for active, rewarding Expeditions.
* **Zen Mode:** Hide all the game elements with one click to get the cleanest, most minimalist to-do app on the market.

#### **Plan 2: The Paid Plan (The "MMO" Online Layer)**

The subscription (our `Social Plan`) isn't for power. It's to **unlock the online world**:

* Cloud Backup & Multi-platform Sync (Android, iOS, Web, Steam, Smartwatch).
* Social Hub (Friends, Chat, Guilds).
* Visit your friends' `Cozy Rooms` (`Animal Crossing`-style).
* P2P Marketplace (`Path of Exile`-style economy).
* `Body Doubling` (Focus Parties) & Co-op `Task Raids`.
* Future Features: `AI Assistant`, `Fog Map` (`Walkscape`-style), `Study Hub` (`Duolingo`-style), and more.
