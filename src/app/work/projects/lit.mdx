<!-- A Grande Visão -->
<h3><strong>What is LIT? (The Grand Vision)</strong></h3>
<p>
  LIT is a hybrid project: a full-featured productivity app and a deep 2D game, all in one. It's built on two layers:
</p>

<h4><strong>Plan 1: The Free Plan (A 100% OFFLIaE Single-Player Game)</strong></h4>
<p>This is not a "demo." It's a complete, fast, and private game that is 100% free, forever.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Full PIM:</strong> A robust task app with sub-tasks, notes, native calendar sync, a flexible Pomodoro timer, and reminders.</li>
  <li><strong>Stats (<code>Solo Leveling</code>):</strong> Track your real-life activities (exercise, study, sleep) to level up your Avatar's core stats (<code>STR</code>, <code>INT</code>, <code>AGI</code>, <code>VIT</code>).</li>
  <li><strong>Idle Hub (<code>Melvor</code> + <code>Gladiatus</code>):</strong> Use "Idle Fuel" (from tasks) for passive skilling (Mining, Crafting) and "Focus Tickets" (from Pomodoro) for active, rewarding Expeditions.</li>
  <li><strong>Zen Mode:</strong> Hide all the game elements with one click to get the cleanest, most minimalist to-do app on the market.</li>
</ul>

<h4><strong>Plan 2: The Paid Plan (The "MMO" Online Layer)</strong></h4>
<p>The subscription (our `Social Plan`) isn't for power. It's to **unlock the online world**:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Cloud Backup & Multi-platform Sync (Android, iOS, Web, Steam, Smartwatch).</li>
  <li>Social Hub (Friends, Chat, Guilds).</li>
  <li>Visit your friends' `Cozy Rooms` (<code>Animal Crossing</code>-style).</li>
  <li>P2P Marketplace (<code>Path of Exile</code>-style economy).</li>
  <li>`Body Doubling` (Focus Parties) & Co-op `Task Raids`.</li>
  <li>Future Features: `AI Assistant`, `Fog Map` (`Walkscape`-style), `Study Hub` (`Duolingo`-style), and more.</li>
</ul>
